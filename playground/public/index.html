IXIA writes the first reply to new clients of law firms and clinics. Guardrails checks each draft against the firm's own knowledge base, Llama Guard 3 and an independent verifier model. Safe drafts are sent at once. Sensitive ones go to a review queue, and the client gets an instant acknowledgement in their own language.

      How the gate decides
      
      
      Five stages, three models, one deterministic rule set. Any block stops the draft; any review holds it; otherwise it is sent.
      
    
      
    
        
      
      1. 01
        Inbound rules
        Prompt-injection markers and sensitive personal data (health, criminal matters, migration status, children, violence or self-harm, religion, trade union) in six languages. The draft model flags the same categories as a second opinion.
        regex + draft model
        2. 
      
      2. 02
        Retrieve
        Multilingual embeddings pick the four most relevant entries of the firm's knowledge base. A Dutch question finds the English fee entry.
        @cf/baai/bge-m3
        3. 
      
      3. 03
        Draft
        A grounded first reply in the client's language, with citations to the knowledge entries it used.
        @cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast
        4. 
      
      4. 04
        Safety and verification, in parallel
        Llama Guard 3 classifies the reply (S6 specialised advice and S7 privacy hold it; other hazards block it). A second model from another family lists every claim the knowledge base does not support, case-specific advice, promised outcomes and echoed sensitive details.
        @cf/meta/llama-guard-3-8b · @cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b
        5. 
      
      5. 05
        Deterministic outbound rules
        Every price or percentage must appear in the retrieved knowledge. Phone numbers, e-mails and links must be the firm's own. Citations must exist. The reply must be in the client's language. An AI-disclosure line is always appended.
